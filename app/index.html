Sons of the One — Bible study, devotionals & prayer community

“For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God.” — Romans 8:14 (KJV)

Sons of the One is a Christian Bible study community from Sons' of the One LLC, built around sonship and discipleship: walking in the Spirit and not in the flesh. Read the Scriptures, pray together, and grow in your spiritual walk every day.

What you'll find

  • The Holy Bible — read the King James Version (1611) and compare modern translations, with bookmarks, highlights and notes.
  • Daily devotionals & verse of the day — start each day with scripture and reflection.
  • Reading plans — follow a daily Bible reading plan and track your progress.
  • Community prayer wall — share prayer requests and pray for fellow believers.
  • Personal journal — record your spiritual journey, prayers and insights.
  • Scripture memory — memorize verses with review and streaks.
  • Lessons & certificates — teachings on sonship, discipleship and baptism.
  • Bible trivia — daily challenges, multiplayer games and tournaments.
  • Church finder — find a church near you.
